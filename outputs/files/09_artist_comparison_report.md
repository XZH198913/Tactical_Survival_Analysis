## 📄 论文写作建议段落（中英双语）

> The sociological portrait reveals a stark divergence: Path 1 (Material/Haptic) encompasses 
> 114 artists producing 
156 works, with identifiable 'escape elites' 
such as Simmons (5 works) and Koťátková (4 works). In contrast, Path 2 (Semiotic/Cognitive) consists of 
17 artists each contributing exactly one work—
a highly dispersed, opportunistic engagement. The overlap rate of merely 
10.1% confirms a strong **'tactical loyalty'**: nearly 90% of artists remain exclusively 
committed to one ontological logic of escape. This sociological evidence anchors Chapter 5's distinction 
between the 'uprising of flesh' and the 'trap of signs.'

**中文摘要**：路径一（物质与触觉反噬）共有 114 位艺术家，
其中 Simmons (5件)、Koťátková (4件) 等构成了持续深耕的“逃逸精英”节点；路径二（符号与认知故障）则呈现高度分散的特征，
17位艺术家各仅贡献1件作品。两条路径的艺术家重叠率仅为 10.1%，
表明近九成艺术家对特定逃逸本体论存在高度忠诚。这一发现为第五章区分“肉身起义”与“符号陷阱”提供了主体性层面的实证基础。
